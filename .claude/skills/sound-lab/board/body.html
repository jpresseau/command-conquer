Battlefield Sound Board

The game's sound, new and old, side by side: the score at each intensity, and every effect. The new sounds are rendered here by the same code the game runs, with the same stereo placement, room and compressor; the old ones are the live synth they replace. Headphones make the stereo obvious.

Tap any button to start the audio.

Music

Song
Intensity
Calm is the pad and an arpeggio; alert adds half-time drums and a driving bass; battle brings in the full kit, a distorted riff, brass and the tune. In the game the intensity follows the fighting you can hear.

The world

The loops under the guns. Each plays until you press it again; in the game they rise and fall with the weather and with what is moving on the screen.
