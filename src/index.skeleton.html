RED ALERT

Real-Time Strategy

Optional. Point this at conquer.mix, local.mix, temperat.mix and hires.mix from your own copy of Red Alert and the game will draw with the real sprites instead of its own — hires.mix also carries the original title screen, which replaces the one above. Add sounds.mix, speech.mix, allies.mix and russian.mix for the original weapons, EVA and the units' own voices, and scores.mix for the soundtrack. Simplest of all: point this at MAIN.MIX and it finds every one of them itself. Nothing is uploaded — the files are read in your browser.

Optional. The battle is fought on a real map's own terrain, ore and start positions instead of a generated one. Point this at your own MAIN.MIX and it will search it for the scenarios Red Alert shipped with; a loose .mpr or .ini scenario works too, as does an .oramap, or a map's map.bin and map.yaml together. Temperate and snow maps work; load the artwork above as well or there is nothing to draw the cliffs with. Generated battles are landlocked — a real map is the only way to get water, and loading one tells you whether it has a coast you can build a naval yard on.

An original game in the spirit of the mid-90s base-building RTS. Not affiliated with, and containing no assets from, any existing game — everything it ships with is drawn and synthesized in code. The two loaders above are the exception: they read files you already own, in your browser, and nothing is ever uploaded. They are kept, though — the archives and the map you pick are copied into this browser's storage so you only choose them once, and forget these next to the loader erases them again.
