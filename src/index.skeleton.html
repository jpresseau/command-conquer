Controls
Selecting
Left-drag
select your units
Double-click
select every unit of that type on screen
Ctrl+A
select your whole army
0–9
teams — Ctrl assigns, plain selects, Shift adds, Alt selects and centres
N / Shift+N
step to the next / previous unit and look at it
I
select every idle unit and look at one — again for the next
Home
centre on the selection (or on your command yard)
Space
look at the last attack on your base — the message line goes where it is about when tapped, and selects what it names
Ordering
Right-click
context order — enemy: attack · ore: harvest · ground: move
Shift + right-click
one more stop after the ones a unit already has — with A, an attack-move stop
A + right-click
attack-move (engage anything on the way) — with Heavy Bombers, on bare ground: carpet that spot
S
hold position — fire from where you stand, never chase
Right-click radar
order the selection across the map without scrolling
Transports & specialists
Right-click an APC
with infantry selected, they board it
U
unload every selected transport
D
deploy — a Mobile Yard into a Command Yard, a layer's mine, the Bridge Layer's span (the sidebar's button says which)
Right-click a unit
with a Recon Drone selected, it shadows that unit wherever it goes
Delete
scuttle the selected units
Camera & battle
Right-drag
grab the map and drag it (the middle button too) — a right-click that does not drag still orders
Arrow keys
pan the camera (screen edges and the radar work too)
Mouse wheel
zoom toward the cursor — smoothly in 3D
+ / −
zoom in / out; hold to keep going
Middle-drag
3D: turn the camera (across) and lean it (up / down) — or Alt + right-drag; middle-click faces north again
Q / E, PgUp / PgDn
3D: turn, lean; the compass on the battlefield turns you back to north
P
pause — the battle waits, the camera does not; the paused screen sets the speed, 1×, 1.5× or 2×
Ctrl+S
save to the slot in use — three slots; 📂 chooses among them when more than one holds a battle, and this screen resumes any
Esc
cancel a placement, repair, sell or an armed weapon — with nothing armed, leave the battle (press it twice)
