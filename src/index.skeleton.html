Optional. Point this at conquer.mix, local.mix, temperat.mix and hires.mix from your own copy of Red Alert and the game will draw with the real sprites instead of its own — hires.mix also carries the original title screen, which replaces the one above. Add sounds.mix, speech.mix, allies.mix and russian.mix for the original weapons, EVA and the units' own voices, and scores.mix for the soundtrack. Simplest of all: point this at your Red Alert CD image — an .iso of either disc — or at MAIN.MIX, and it finds every archive itself. Nothing is uploaded — the files are read in your browser.

Optional. The battle is fought on a real map's own terrain, ore and start positions instead of a generated one. Point this at a Red Alert .iso or your own MAIN.MIX and it will search for the scenarios Red Alert shipped with; a loose .mpr or .ini scenario works too, as does an .oramap, or a map's map.bin and map.yaml together. Temperate and snow maps work; load the artwork above as well or there is nothing to draw the cliffs with. Generated battles are landlocked — a real map is the only way to get water, and loading one tells you whether it has a coast you can build a naval yard on.

Controls
Selecting
Left-drag
select your units
Double-click
select every unit of that type on screen
Ctrl+A
select your whole army
0–9
teams — Ctrl assigns, plain selects, Shift adds, Alt selects and centres
N / Shift+N
step to the next / previous unit and look at it
Home
centre on the selection (or on your command yard)
Ordering
Right-click
context order — enemy: attack · ore: harvest · ground: move
A + right-click
attack-move (engage anything on the way)
S
hold position — fire from where you stand, never chase
Right-click radar
order the selection across the map without scrolling
Transports & specialists
Right-click an APC
with infantry selected, they board it
U
unload every selected transport
D
deploy — turns a Mobile Yard back into a Command Yard
Delete
scuttle the selected units
Camera & battle
Right-drag
grab the map and drag it (the middle button too) — a right-click that does not drag still orders
Arrow keys
pan the camera (screen edges and the radar work too)
Mouse wheel
zoom toward the cursor — smoothly in 3D
+ / −
zoom in / out; hold to keep going
Middle-drag
3D: turn the camera (across) and lean it (up / down) — or Alt + right-drag; middle-click faces north again
Q / E, PgUp / PgDn
3D: turn, lean; the compass on the battlefield turns you back to north
Ctrl+S
save — resume it from this screen later
Esc
leave the battle
