RC COMMAND

Real-Time Strategy

Vanguard  versus  Redline

Mine Scrap with harvesters, unload it at a refinery for credits, and spend them on power, production and armour. Build out and the roster opens up — attack dogs, engineers and thieves, an APC to carry them, helicopters that need a pad to rearm, and a Mobile Yard to found a second base. Lose your power plants and the whole base slows down. Redline is doing the same thing across the map — and their first attack wave is already on a timer.

Optional. Point this at conquer.mix, local.mix, temperat.mix and hires.mix from your own copy of Red Alert and the game will draw with the real sprites instead of its own. Nothing is uploaded — the files are read in your browser.

Optional. The battle is fought on a real map's own terrain, ore and start positions instead of a generated one. Point this at your own MAIN.MIX and it will search it for the scenarios Red Alert shipped with; a loose .mpr or .ini scenario works too, as does an .oramap, or a map's map.bin and map.yaml together. Temperate maps only, and load the artwork above as well or there is nothing to draw the cliffs with.

An original game in the spirit of the mid-90s base-building RTS. Not affiliated with, and containing no assets from, any existing game — everything it ships with is drawn and synthesized in code. The artwork loader above is the one exception, and it reads files you already own, in your browser, without sending or storing them.
