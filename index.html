RC COMMAND

Real-Time Strategy

Vanguard  versus  Redline

Mine Scrap with harvesters, unload it at a refinery for credits, and spend them on power, production and armour. Redline is doing the same thing across the map — and their first attack wave is already on a timer.

Left-drag
select your units
Right-click
context order — enemy: attack · scrap: harvest · ground: move
A + right-click
attack-move (engage anything on the way)
WASD / arrows
pan the camera (screen edges work too)
Mouse wheel
zoom
Esc
leave the battle

An original game in the spirit of the mid-90s base-building RTS. Not affiliated with, and containing no assets from, any existing game. All art and audio generated in code.
