# Red Alert 2 in Breachwater — every unit and building, by role

The owner asked for all of Red Alert 2's units and buildings (the base game, not Yuri's Revenge),
under Breachwater's own names: the same job and the same mechanic, never RA2's name, art or text.
The Compact (`allied`) takes the Allied roster and the Dominion (`soviet`) the Soviet one.

This page is the map. Each RA2 item is either something Breachwater already has (the key it maps
to) or a new entry with the batch that brings it in. A batch is one mechanic and the things that
need it, shipped with its tests. When a batch lands, its rows gain the new key, and the batch gets
a section below with its numbers.

## Batch 1: the Lens (`core/lens.js`, `sprites/lens.js`)

**Lens Tower** (`prism`, Compact, 1x2, 1200, -75 power, needs a Radar Post, dark without power):
`lensbeam`, 110 damage, range 19, a shot every 3 s, weak against concrete (0.6). Alone it is a
little over half an Arc Tower. When it fires, every idle Lens Tower of the same side within 8
cells of it, or of a tower already relaying, relays into the shot: +50% per relay, at most 6, so
a full cluster fires four times its own shot. Each relay spends a full reload, which is the
bargain: a cluster is one enormous gun, not several ordinary ones. A tower still being built or
already reloading does not relay, and cuts the line behind it.

**Lens Tank** (`prismtank`, Compact, 1200, needs a Research Lab, 240 hp, light armour):
`lenssplit`, 75 damage at range 22, past a Battle Tank's 18. The beam forks onto the 4 nearest
enemies within 3 cells of its target, each taking half the shot through its own armour; never a
friend, an aircraft, a submerged boat, a passenger or a wall.

Both draw a `beam` effect, a hot core inside a soft glow gone in 0.35 s, and neither has a muzzle
flash. The beam has its own sound (`beam` in `audio/recipes.js`). The opponent builds Lens Towers
as the Compact's premium defence (ratio 0.10, at most 3, beside the Arc Tower in the plan) and
buys the Lens Tank from 2200 credits. `unit/lens` holds the rules, `e2e/lens` the picture.

## Batch 2: the Arc (`core/arc.js`, `sprites/arc.js`)

**Arc Trooper** (`arctroop`, Dominion infantry, 500, needs a Radar Post, 130 hp, light armour):
`arcgun`, 30 damage at range 9, a shot every 1.3 s, as good against a tank (1.0) as against a man.
Standing within 3 cells of one of its side's Arc Towers, on foot, it **charges** the tower: a
charged tower fires through a brownout, which an uncharged one never does, and each trooper adds
a quarter of a shot, up to 3 of them. Each charged shot draws an arc from every charging trooper
up to the coil. The Arc Tower's rules name what charges it (`chargedBy:'arctroop'`), so the Lens
Tower is not charged by them.

**Arc Tank** (`arctank`, Dominion, 1000, needs a Radar Post, 380 hp, heavy armour): `arcbolt`,
55 damage at range 13. The bolt jumps from its target to the 2 nearest enemies within 2.5 cells,
each taking 60% through its own armour. It uses the Lens Tank's fork with its own numbers
(`split:{ n, cells, share }` on the weapon).

The Arc Tower's own bolt is drawn and heard as an arc now too (`shot:'arc'`, the `zap` sound),
where it used to be a machine-gun tracer. An arc is the beam effect broken into seven jagged
legs, re-thrown every 0.05 s so it crackles, and none of the three has a muzzle flash. The
opponent's infantry mix buys Arc Troopers from 1000 credits and its vehicle mix Arc Tanks from
1700. `unit/arc` holds the rules and `e2e/arc` the picture.

## Batch 3: towns, garrisons and digging in (`core/garrison.js`, `core/dig.js`, `sprites/town.js`, `render3d/dig3d.js`)

**Towns** are a skirmish setup option, TOWNS (`town` in `rules/skirmish.js`), off by default, so a
daily, a mission and every recorded battle are the battle they were. With it on, two towns stand
in the contested ground on a standard map (scaled with the map's size), each a few of the town's
buildings: a **Cottage** (`cottage`, 2x2, holds 3 squads), an **Apartment Block** (`flats`,
2x3, holds 5) and a **Town Hall** (`hall`, 3x3, holds 6, one per battle). None stands within 22
cells of a start. They are laid from their own random stream after the bases, so the terrain,
the ore and the bases are exactly the map without them. They belong to the `civ` seat, which has
team -1, no brain and no turn, and is hostile to nobody.

**Garrisons.** Right-click a house with infantry selected (a squad with a gun, not a dog) and up
to its number of squads go in through the transport's own order. The house takes their side: its
roof wears their colour, the enemy can shoot it, and each squad inside fires from it with its own
weapon, out to its range plus 2 cells. UNLOAD walks them out, a house that falls spills them,
and fire clears it: a weapon marked `clears` (flame, grenades, the Flame Tower) that hits a held
house burns every squad inside for half the hit. When the last squad leaves, the house is the
town's again. A held house is never sold, repaired, captured or rebuilt, and keeps no side alive.
The opponent sends its idle squads, two or more, into the nearest house within 34 cells of its
start, every 8 s.

**Digging in** (the GI's deploy): DEPLOY on a Compact Rifle Squad (`dig:'allied'`) says DIG IN.
The squad crouches behind a ring of sandbags; it cannot move, takes half of every hit, hits 1.4x
as hard and reloads in three quarters of the time. DEPLOY again, any move order, or a board
order climbs it out.

`unit/garrison` (24), `unit/dig` (12) and `e2e/garrison` (15) hold it.

## Batch 4: mind control (`core/mind.js`, `sprites/mind.js`)

**Mentalist** (`mentalist`, Dominion infantry, 1200, after a Research Lab, 120 hp): its
`mindlink` does no damage. The unit it reaches, at range 13 every 3.5 s, changes side: it drops
its order, leaves its old team and the old side's selection, and its passengers come with it. A
Mentalist holds one unit at a time, so taking a second frees the first, and when the Mentalist
dies what it held goes home. It never takes a building, an aircraft, another Mentalist or a
Drone Tank (`mindproof`). `_rtsWeaponReaches` says so, so it is never aimed at one. The hold is
plain data on the unit (`mind:{ by, was }`), so a save keeps it. The link is drawn violet.

**Drone Tank** (`dronetank`, Compact, 900, after a Relay Hub, 340 hp, heavy armour): fast, hovers
over water like a Hovercraft, and is `mindproof`. Its gun does 26 damage at range 16. It runs
from a **Relay Hub** (`relayhub`, Compact, 2x2, 600, -50 power, after a Vehicle Works). While its
side has no finished hub with full power, every Drone Tank is offline: it stops and holds its
fire, and its readout says so.

**Echo Sensor** (`echo`, Dominion, 2x2, 1000, -60 power, after a Radar Post): `detects` 16 cells,
the Destroyer's sonar on land. It finds a submerged boat (through `_rtsCloakAI`, as sonar does)
and unmasks a jammed unit (`core/jammer.js`).

The opponent builds a hub and a sensor after its defences, and buys Mentalists from 2000 credits
and Drone Tanks from 1500. `unit/mind` (20) and `e2e/mind` (8) hold it.

## Batch 5: the Rift (`core/rift.js`, `sprites/rift.js`, `render3d/veil3d.js`)

**Rift Trooper** (`rifttroop`, Compact infantry, 1500, after a Research Lab, 120 hp): it does not
walk. A move order charges a jump for 1.2 s where it stands, and then it is on the open cell
nearest the spot, having crossed no ground; it takes no new order while it charges. Its `riftgun`
(range 12, every 1 s) does no damage. Each shot pushes the target 60 further out of time: frozen,
it does not move or fire, and nothing else can hurt it. When the push passes its hit points it is
gone, with no wreck. Let the shooting lapse for 2.5 s and it comes back, whole. The beam is drawn
pale blue-white.

**Rift Harvester** (`riftharv`, Compact, 1800, after a Research Lab, 600 hp, heavy armour, holds
980): it mines like any harvester, and when its hopper is full it jumps to its refinery's dock
after 1.5 s instead of driving there (`core/harvest.js` asks `_rtsRiftHome`).

**Veil Tank** (`veiltank`, Compact, 1100, after a Research Lab, 300 hp): once it has stood still
for 2 s without firing, the jammer's own rules apply to it alone (`core/jammer.js` `_rtsJammed`).
It is not targeted from range, and is found by an enemy standing close, a Spotter or an Echo
Sensor. To an enemy who has not found it, it is drawn as a tree. Moving or firing gives it away.
Its `veilgun` is a heavy, slow shell (85 damage at range 17, every 2.6 s).

The opponent buys Rift Troopers from 2200 credits and Veil Tanks from 1800. `unit/rift` (20) and
`e2e/rift` (8) hold it.

## Batch 6: explosives (`core/blast.js`, `sprites/blast.js`, `render3d/blast3d.js`)

**Sapper** (`sapper`, Dominion infantry, 600, after a Radar Post, 100 hp): its `satchel` does no
damage when it lands. It leaves a charge on the target (`e.bomb`, plain data, so a save keeps
it), on anything on the ground: a tank, a building or a ship at the quay. The charge goes off
after 5 s for 560 to the target and half that to everything within 1.6 cells, whoever's it is.
A target carries one charge at a time, so `_rtsWeaponReaches` says a charged one is not worth
another and the Sapper moves on. If the target is destroyed first, the charge goes off where it
fell. A charge blinks a red light over what it is on, faster as the fuse runs down, and a
selected unit's readout counts it down.

**Zealot** (`zealot`, Dominion infantry, 300, after a Barracks, 70 hp) and **Wrecker** (`wrecker`,
Dominion, 1500, after a Vehicle Works and a Radar Post, 320 hp, a lorry of drums): each one's
shot is the explosion. The Zealot's is 260 within 2 cells and the Wrecker's 900 within 3.5. The
blast falls off evenly to the edge, measured to a building's edge, and hurts friend and foe
alike. The carrier goes with it, and since it carries the explosive, killing it sets it off where
it falls (`core/capture.js` `_rtsKill`). It goes off once only, and no crew climbs out of a
Wrecker.

**Frogman** (`frogman`, Compact infantry, 1000, after a Research Lab, 120 hp): he moves through
the hovercraft's domain, so he swims as well as he walks, and is drawn to the chest in the water.
His machine pistol is for men. His `limpet` goes only on a ship or a building, for 600 after
3 s. `_rtsPickWeapon` now scores a weapon that cannot reach the target as nothing, so he answers
infantry with the pistol and a ship with the limpet.

The opponent does not buy any of the four. A Zealot or a Wrecker kills its own side's men round
it, so an opponent that buys them without a plan for where to send them only shuffles its own
battle: bought even from 2000 credits, they moved `unit/endgame`, `unit/aitactics` and
`unit/allycmd`. The opponent's use of them is batch 12. `unit/blast` (22) and `e2e/blast` (10) hold it.

## Batch 7: radiation (`core/rad.js`, `sprites/rad.js`, `render3d/rad3d.js`)

A **pool** of radiation (`G.rads`, plain data, so a save keeps it) is a circle of poisoned ground
that fades to nothing over its life. Every 0.5 s, everything on the ground inside one is hurt by
its strength, falling off to half at the edge. Infantry take all of it, vehicles and ships 0.3 of
it, and buildings and aircraft none, friend and foe alike, except a `radproof` unit. Pools close
together that one side made merge into one, made stronger and longer, so a Blight Trooper firing
at one spot does not lay a hundred. Poisoned ground glows green, breathing slowly.

**Blight Trooper** (`blight`, Dominion infantry, 600, after a Research Lab, 140 hp, radproof): its
green ray (45 at range 11, every 1.6 s, made for infantry) leaves a pool of 1.3 cells for 5 s
where it hits. DEPLOY (the button says IRRADIATE, then STOP) stands it still through the dig-in
order, and the ground within 3 cells of it burns at 26 a second for as long as it stands. Any
move order stops it.

**Fission Reactor** (`reactor`, Dominion, 3x3, 1000, after a Research Lab, 900 hp): 400 power,
twice a Fusion Plant's for twice its price. Destroyed, it blasts what is round it (200 within
4 cells) and melts down: a pool of 7 cells at 22 a second, fading over 40 s. A reactor that is
sold does neither.

The opponent builds a reactor late in its plan. It does not buy the Blight Trooper, whose
deployed ground burns its own side as well: that is batch 12, with the explosives.
`unit/rad` (17) and `e2e/rad` (12) hold it.

## Batch 8: aloft (`core/aloft.js`, `sprites/aloft.js`)

**Endless** aircraft (`endless`) carry what they fire for as long as they fly: `core/move.js`
never sends one home to a pad, and `core/combat.js` never counts its rounds, so it neither runs
dry nor crashes for want of a pad.

**Leviathan** (`leviathan`, Dominion, 2000, after an Airfield and a Research Lab, 1500 hp, heavy
armour, speed 5, endless): an armoured airship. Its `levbomb` (range 3, every 2.2 s) drops a bomb
from where it is, through the Heavy Bomber's falling bombs (`G.bombs`). The bomb bursts after 1 s
for 200, falling off evenly to nothing at 2.2 cells, on everything under it, whoever's it is.
Two engine pods turn their props.

**Falcon** (`falcon`, Compact, 1600, after a Helipad and a Radar Post, 260 hp, speed 34): a delta
strike jet with two heavy bombs (130, splash 2.6, best against buildings), then home to a pad,
leaving a contrail.

**Jetpack Trooper** (`jetpack`, Compact infantry, 600, after a Radar Post, 90 hp, endless): it
flies at altitude 7, straight over water, walls and cliffs. Only anti-air can hit it, and a tank
cannot run it down (`core/units.js` `_rtsOverrun` skips anything in the air).

**Carrier** (`carrier`, Compact ship, 2000, after a Naval Yard and a Research Lab, 900 hp): no
gun of its own. Its `hornets` (range 34, every 2 s) launches a **Hornet** (`hornet`) at the
target, with up to 3 in the air at once. A Hornet is a drone with 4 missiles. With its rack
empty or its target gone, it flies home and lands on its Carrier, which counts it back in. With
no Carrier, it falls out of the sky. A Hornet is never built (`spawned`): `core/production.js`
will not queue one and `ui/sidebar.js` does not show one.

The opponent does not buy any of them. It has no team that flies a Leviathan or a Falcon or sails
a Carrier, so it would buy them and leave them at home (`unit/aiplan`). Jetpack Troopers in its
infantry ran Mirror Line past its gold par (491 s against 480). Its use of them is batch 12.
`unit/aloft` (21) and `e2e/aloft` (6) hold it.

## Batch 9: the deep (`core/deep.js`, `sprites/deep.js`)

**Kraken** (`kraken`, Dominion, 1000, after a shipyard and a Research Lab, 500 hp): it runs
submerged as a submarine does (`cloak`). Its `tentacles` (range 2.5) take hold of a ship
(`e.grip` and the ship's `e.gripBy`, plain data, so a save keeps them). It surfaces, and the ship
does nothing while it is held (`core/tick.js` skips it). It cannot move or fire, and is crushed
for 55 a second through its armour. The two are locked together: the crowd's shove
(`core/move.js` `_rtsSeparate`) does not part them. The Kraken lets go when the ship is gone,
when the Kraken dies, or when they drift apart. It never takes hold of anything ashore.

**Sonar Dolphin** (`dolphin`, Compact, 600, after a shipyard and a Radar Post, 200 hp, fast): it
hears submerged boats and Krakens within 8 cells (`detects`, the Destroyer's sonar), and its sonic
burst (45, range 9, an arc) reaches anything afloat or below.

**Sea Hornet** (`seahornet`, Dominion, 700, after a shipyard, 350 hp): a fast flak boat whose
`scorpflak` reaches aircraft and infantry ashore. **Bombard** (`bombard`, Dominion, 2200, after
a shipyard and a Research Lab, 1300 hp): a missile battleship with two 150 missiles at range 40,
beyond the Cruiser's 38.

The opponent does not buy them yet; that is batch 12. `unit/deep` (15) and `e2e/deep` (6) hold
it.
