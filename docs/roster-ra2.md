# Red Alert 2 in Breachwater — every unit and building, by role

The owner asked for all of Red Alert 2's units and buildings (the base game, not Yuri's Revenge),
under Breachwater's own names: the same job and the same mechanic, never RA2's name, art or text.
The Compact (`allied`) takes the Allied roster and the Dominion (`soviet`) the Soviet one.

This page is the map. Each RA2 item is either something Breachwater already has (the key it maps
to) or a new entry with the batch that brings it in. A batch is one mechanic and the things that
need it, shipped with its tests. When a batch lands, its rows gain the new key, and the batch gets
a section below with its numbers.

## Batch 1: the Lens (`core/lens.js`, `sprites/lens.js`)

**Lens Tower** (`prism`, Compact, 1x2, 1200, -75 power, needs a Radar Post, dark without power):
`lensbeam`, 110 damage, range 19, a shot every 3 s, weak against concrete (0.6). Alone it is a
little over half an Arc Tower. When it fires, every idle Lens Tower of the same side within 8
cells of it, or of a tower already relaying, relays into the shot: +50% per relay, at most 6, so
a full cluster fires four times its own shot. Each relay spends a full reload, which is the
bargain: a cluster is one enormous gun, not several ordinary ones. A tower still being built or
already reloading does not relay, and cuts the line behind it.

**Lens Tank** (`prismtank`, Compact, 1200, needs a Research Lab, 240 hp, light armour):
`lenssplit`, 75 damage at range 22, past a Battle Tank's 18. The beam forks onto the 4 nearest
enemies within 3 cells of its target, each taking half the shot through its own armour; never a
friend, an aircraft, a submerged boat, a passenger or a wall.

Both draw a `beam` effect, a hot core inside a soft glow gone in 0.35 s, and neither has a muzzle
flash. The beam has its own sound (`beam` in `audio/recipes.js`). The opponent builds Lens Towers
as the Compact's premium defence (ratio 0.10, at most 3, beside the Arc Tower in the plan) and
buys the Lens Tank from 2200 credits. `unit/lens` holds the rules, `e2e/lens` the picture.

## Batch 2: the Arc (`core/arc.js`, `sprites/arc.js`)

**Arc Trooper** (`arctroop`, Dominion infantry, 500, needs a Radar Post, 130 hp, light armour):
`arcgun`, 30 damage at range 9, a shot every 1.3 s, as good against a tank (1.0) as against a man.
Standing within 3 cells of one of its side's Arc Towers, on foot, it **charges** the tower: a
charged tower fires through a brownout, which an uncharged one never does, and each trooper adds
a quarter of a shot, up to 3 of them. Each charged shot draws an arc from every charging trooper
up to the coil. The Arc Tower's rules name what charges it (`chargedBy:'arctroop'`), so the Lens
Tower is not charged by them.

**Arc Tank** (`arctank`, Dominion, 1000, needs a Radar Post, 380 hp, heavy armour): `arcbolt`,
55 damage at range 13. The bolt jumps from its target to the 2 nearest enemies within 2.5 cells,
each taking 60% through its own armour. It uses the Lens Tank's fork with its own numbers
(`split:{ n, cells, share }` on the weapon).

The Arc Tower's own bolt is drawn and heard as an arc now too (`shot:'arc'`, the `zap` sound),
where it used to be a machine-gun tracer. An arc is the beam effect broken into seven jagged
legs, re-thrown every 0.05 s so it crackles, and none of the three has a muzzle flash. The
opponent's infantry mix buys Arc Troopers from 1000 credits and its vehicle mix Arc Tanks from
1700. `unit/arc` holds the rules and `e2e/arc` the picture.
